## A) LinkedIn Company Page

**Company name:** Open Spot

**Tagline:** Remplissez vos annulations de rendez-vous par SMS.

**About**

Open Spot aide les commerces à rendez-vous — salons, barbiers, spas et cliniques beauté — à récupérer des revenus perdus lors d'annulations de dernière minute.

Le produit alerte par SMS les clients qui ont accepté une liste d'attente, recueille les réponses et laisse le commerce confirmer manuellement le rendez-vous. Open Spot complète le système de réservation existant; il ne le remplace pas.

## F) Startup/local directories

**Short listing description**

Open Spot (2e Chance RDV) — SMS alerts to fill last-minute appointment cancellations for salons, barbers, spas, and beauty clinics.

**Long listing description**

Open Spot is a focused SaaS layer for appointment-based businesses in Québec and Canada. When a slot opens due to a cancellation, merchants can alert opted-in clients by SMS, review replies, and manually confirm the appointment. The product works alongside existing booking systems and does not auto-confirm clients.

**Tags/categories**

SaaS, SMS, appointments, beauty, salons, Québec, customer retention

**Founder/product blurb**

We built Open Spot to help local appointment businesses recover revenue from late cancellations without adding another full booking platform.
