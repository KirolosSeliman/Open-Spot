# Backlink Targets

Legitimate backlink opportunities for Open Spot. Do not buy links, spam comments, or submit to low-quality AI directories.

Canonical site: https://open-spot.ca

---

## Owned profiles

**Why it matters:** You control naming, URL, and anchor text on properties you own.

**Recommended target page:** https://open-spot.ca (homepage) or https://open-spot.ca/how-it-works

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot, Open Spot — 2e Chance RDV

**Quality rules:** One official website URL; consistent logo and description.

**Rejection rules:** No `*.vercel.app` links; no conflicting product descriptions.

**What not to do:** Do not create duplicate profiles with different brand names.
