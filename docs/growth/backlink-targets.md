# Backlink target strategy

Legitimate backlink acquisition only. No spam, no purchased low-quality links, no fake press.

Official site: https://open-spot.ca

## Owned profiles

**Why it matters:** Search engines use consistent entity signals across profiles you control.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca` or `https://open-spot.ca/about`

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot; Open Spot — 2e Chance RDV

**Quality rules**

- Use the same logo and description everywhere.
- Link to the canonical domain only.

**Rejection rules**

- Do not create profiles with fake addresses or fake team members.

**What not to do**

- Do not claim rankings, awards, or client logos without proof.

## GitHub

**Why it matters:** Developer credibility and a stable branded link from a high-trust domain.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca`

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot

**Quality rules**

- Keep README honest and concise.
- Add topics relevant to the product.

**What not to do**

- Do not add fake stars, badges, or "used by thousands" claims.

## LinkedIn

**Why it matters:** Brand entity clarity for B2B SaaS and Québec local businesses.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca/contact` or homepage

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot

**Quality rules**

- Company page copy should match the product.
- Mention manual confirmation and SMS consent.

**What not to do**

- Do not invent employees, clients, or case studies.

## Google Business Profile

**Why it matters:** Local discovery for Québec service businesses when appropriate.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca`

**Suggested anchor text:** site officiel Open Spot

**Quality rules**

- Use service-area business settings if no public storefront exists.
- Only create if the business is real.

**What not to do**

- Do not use a fake physical address.

## Local Québec / Montréal business directories

**Why it matters:** Reinforces local relevance for salons, barbers, spas, and clinics in Québec.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca/solution-annulations-rdv`

**Suggested anchor text:** remplir les annulations de rendez-vous par SMS

**Quality rules**

- Choose directories with editorial standards.
- Use accurate French descriptions.

**What not to do**

- Do not submit to irrelevant foreign directories in bulk.

## Startup directories

**Why it matters:** Discovery among founders and early adopters.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca/about`

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot — 2e Chance RDV

**Quality rules**

## Future client websites

**Why it matters:** Highly relevant industry links when clients voluntarily mention their tools.

**Recommended target page:** `https://open-spot.ca`

**Suggested anchor text:** Open Spot

**Quality rules**

- Ask only after a real relationship exists.
- Let the client choose placement and wording.

**What not to do**

- Do not offer incentives for links.
- Do not require clients to link as a condition of service unless contractually real and ethical.

## Relevant blog / resource pages

**Why it matters:** Editorial mentions from useful content outperform spammy links.

**Recommended target page:**

- `https://open-spot.ca/resources/liste-attente-sms-salons`
- `https://open-spot.ca/guides/remplir-annulations-rdv-sms`

**Suggested anchor text:** liste d'attente SMS pour salons

**Quality rules**

- Target pages that already discuss no-shows, cancellations, or salon operations.
- Suggest a resource that helps their readers.

**What not to do**

- Do not post comment spam.
- Do not buy private blog network links.
- Do not fabricate press releases.
